## What is Revix Systems

We Revix Systems are a Developer Team that develops app in C#. These apps are for everybody and they range from a Game Manager to a Video Downloader.

We are special because we depend on our community, our projects would not stand here how they are now if our community did not test them. Therefor we are special because we rely on our community to give us feedback on our Projects and what to improve.
